## Logo guidance

https://brand-experience.ieee.org/ieee-brand/frequently-asked-questions/

The Student Branches and Chapters FAQ calls for Formata/Calibri, approved IEEE colors, and a horizontal or vertical divider between the master brand and branch name. The master brand must not be manipulated. Website typography is separate from the lockup lettering.

https://brand-experience.ieee.org/guidelines/master-brand-and-logos/

The official download page states that downloading accepts the toolkit agreement:
https://brand-experience.ieee.org/guidelines/brand-identity/toolkit-agreement/

The user authorized download and accepted the toolkit terms. Official blue PNG and vector PDF were downloaded through the signed-in browser. The PDF was converted to SVG paths without changing proportions or path geometry. The white variants use the permitted white master brand treatment. Horizontal and stacked lockups use the full university name and Student Branch with a divider.

Export files: public/assets/ieee-alu-{horizontal,stacked}-{blue,white}.svg. These are editable SVGs with vector master artwork and live university-name text. The lettering specifies Calibri with Arial as a viewing fallback; install/use Calibri before producing final print outlines. This design has not been submitted to IEEE for formal review.

The website uses the horizontal blue lockup. The official master mark remains upright and undistorted in the site header and footer.

## Faculty petition roles

Checked IEEE MGA's official formation policies through the in-app browser. Student branch petition endorsers are one institutional department head, one faculty counselor, and two faculty members. The counselor and two faculty endorsers must hold active IEEE Graduate Student Member grade or higher and teach in IEEE fields of interest. The department head need not hold IEEE membership but needs an IEEE web account. The petition requires 12 eligible student/graduate student member signatures and approval by the Region Director (or designee), Regional Student Activities Chair, and Section Chair. Chapter advisors are separate from the two branch faculty endorsers. Leadership placeholders reflect these distinctions; no names or formal appointments are inferred.

Source: https://mga.ieee.org/volunteer-hub/geographic-unit-operations/formations-and-petitions#student
